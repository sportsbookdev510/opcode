Wallets and sign-in

Your wallet is your account. There is no email or password. Swaps use your wallet directly; liquidity provision is optional. This page covers which wallets work, what you are asked to sign and when, and what opcode will never ask for.

Supported wallets

Phantom, Solflare and Backpack, on Solana mainnet. On a computer, install the browser extension. On a phone, opcode has to run inside the wallet's own browser: if your phone browser has no wallet, the dialog offers Open in Phantom, Open in Solflare and Open in Backpack, which reopen this page in that wallet. On a computer, if the dialog says no supported wallet was detected, the extension is what is missing.

Hardware wallets that connect over USB are not supported directly. Use one through a supported wallet if that wallet offers it.

Robinhood Chain

The same dialog connects an Ethereum wallet on Robinhood Chain, such as MetaMask or Backpack, for Robinhood's stock tokens. Choose the wallet under Robinhood Chain and approve the connection. If the wallet is on another network, it asks to switch to Robinhood Chain, chain ID 4663, and to add the network first if it does not have it. The dialog then shows the address and its ETH balance on Robinhood Chain.

Connecting asks for no signature and moves nothing. This browser remembers the wallet until you choose Disconnect, and it stays connected whether or not you are signed in with a Solana wallet. The wallet page then shows its Robinhood tokens and USDG with the rest.

Signing in

  1. Ask for a quote

    Looking at markets, and a Quick ticket's estimate, never need a wallet. While you are signed out, Connect wallet to swap, Get firm quote and, in Pools, Connect to continue open the Sign in with a wallet dialog.

  2. Choose a wallet and an account

    If your wallet exposes more than one account, pick the one you want to trade from.

  3. Approve the message

    Your wallet shows a message that names this site, your address, a one-time code and an expiry, and the sentence “Sign in to Opcode. This request does not authorize a transaction or transfer any assets.” Signing it proves the address is yours. It costs nothing and moves nothing.

You stay signed in on this browser for seven days. If you switch accounts inside your wallet, opcode signs you out and asks again, so a quote can never be built for one account and signed by another.

What you will sign

You are shownWhenWhat it does
A sign-in messageOnce per browser, every seven daysProves you control the address. Authorizes nothing.
A swap transactionEach time you sign a quoteOn OPmode, moves exactly the two amounts on the review panel. On Quick, spends your input and receives at least the minimum shown. Plus the network fee.
A pool transactionOnly if you deposit to or withdraw from a poolMoves both assets within the maximums or minimums you reviewed, in exchange for pool shares or back from them.
A launch transactionOnly if you create or trade a token on Launch, or claim its rewardsDoes what the Launch page showed you before you approved it. See How a launch works.
An approval, on Robinhood ChainBefore a swap, when your wallet has not already allowed itLets KyberSwap's router spend exactly the amount you are paying, and nothing more.
A swap, on Robinhood ChainEach time you swap a Robinhood tokenPays the amount you entered and receives at least 99.5% of the estimate, or reverts. Plus the network fee in ETH.
What opcode never asks for

Your seed phrase, your private key, an approval for more than the amount you are swapping, or a signature on anything other than the items above. Nobody from opcode will message you first. If a page or a person asks for any of these, it is not opcode.

Balances

Your wallet page, the address in the bar, shows what your address holds on both products: stock tokens, launch tokens, pool shares, SOL for fees and USDC, each with its value, and the total. Balances are read from the chain at a single slot, with the time they were read. If a consistent reading cannot be obtained, the balance is shown as unavailable rather than guessed, and the page says how many holdings have no current price.

Stock token balances are shown in raw tokens, with the equivalent in scaled units, which count shares, beneath. Dividends, splits and the multiplier explains the difference.

Signing out

Choose your wallet address at the top right of the terminal, then Sign out. The session is revoked on the server, not merely forgotten by your browser.

Go deeperHow the sign-in is verified

opcode uses Sign-In With Solana where the wallet supports it and a signed text message otherwise. The server issues a challenge bound to your browser, valid for five minutes and usable once. It verifies the Ed25519 signature against your address, checks that the domain and address inside the signed text are the ones it issued, and only then opens a session in an HttpOnly cookie that scripts on the page cannot read. Requests that change anything must also come from this site's own origin.
