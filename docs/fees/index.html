Understand the price

Fees and limits

What each route charges, stated before you sign. Plus what the network charges, which opcode does not receive.

Quick

opcode platform fee0%
Trading feethe route's own fee, as quoted by Jupiter, shown on the review before you sign
Slippage tolerancefixed at 0.50%; the minimum received reflects it

The OPmode fee

Rate5 basis points, which is 0.05%
Charged onthe amount you pay
Paid inthe asset you pay with: USDC on a buy, the token on a sell
Roundingup to the next smallest unit
Where it goes80% stays in the pool for its liquidity providers; 20% goes to the protocol fee vault
Shownon the review panel, before you sign

The fee is included, not added. If you choose to pay 100 USDC, exactly 100 USDC leaves your wallet: 0.05 is the trading fee and 99.95 buys tokens. One basis point is one hundredth of a percent.

opcode's protocol fee revenue goes to buying $OP back on the open market.

The program enforces the rate

The settlement program recomputes the fee from the signed amount and the market's configured rate, and rejects a quote whose fee differs by a single unit in either direction. It also splits the fee itself: the liquidity providers' 80%, rounded down, goes into the pool's vault and the rest into the fee vault. The rate is capped in the program at 1% and can be changed only while a market is paused; changing it voids every outstanding quote.

The spread is not a fee

On OPmode you usually buy slightly above the reference and sell slightly below it. That difference is the pool's price for taking the other side of your trade. It varies with size, with the feed's confidence and with the pool's inventory, and the inventory adjustment can move either price across the reference. The review panel's Price row is the amount paid divided by the amount received, fee included, with its distance from the reference in basis points, so that distance includes the 5 bps fee as well as the spread. The fee rate does not change with size or inventory.

Token account rentabout 0.002 SOLthe first time you hold a given tokenlocked in your own account; you recover it by closing the account

The Quick review shows an estimate of the network fee and account rent before you sign. Keep about 0.01 SOL in your wallet and you will not run short.

What there is not

  • No additional protocol fee for an LP deposit or withdrawal; network fees and account rent still apply.
  • No fee for a quote you do not take, or one that expires.
  • No fee for holding, and no inactivity fee.
  • Nothing to buy, stake or hold for a better rate. The fee is the same for everyone, and trading never requires $OP.
