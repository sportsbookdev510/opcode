Trade

The terminal

Markets lists every stock token; each stock has its own page with a ticket beside it. Two routes fill an order: Quick, an estimated swap through Jupiter, and OPmode, a firm quote from opcode's own pool on the ten markets that have one.

Quick and OPmode

Quick supports the listed xStocks and Backpack Securities tokens when a route is available: review the expected output, minimum received, slippage tolerance and fees before signing. OPmode is offered on NVDAx, SPYx, QQQx, GLDx, TSLAx, AAPLx, MSFTx, METAx, COINx and CRCLx, where opcode runs its own pool: a firm quote with exact input, output, fee and expiry, signed for your wallet, and no slippage setting because nothing can move. OPmode is priced from the underlying stock or ETF on Pyth, pays and receives USDC, and quotes only while a US market session is open, so not at weekends or on exchange holidays. Each order takes one route. The ticket lists both and picks OPmode where its pool quotes and the order fits its largest order, Quick otherwise; you can pick the other route yourself.

The ten original xStocks OPmode markets are currently paused. A Backpack market can appear in OPmode after its pool is explicitly configured and funded and its required reference checks pass. Listing a token does not create or activate a pool; current availability is shown on each stock's ticket.

Markets

Markets lists one row per share. Where more than one issuer lists a share (xStocks and Backpack Securities on Solana, Robinhood on Robinhood Chain), the row names every token and issuer and opens the first, xStocks before Backpack before Robinhood. A Robinhood token's page links to the same share on Solana. Each row shows the price, the 24-hour move, the last 24 hours as a line, and the token's gap to its share where the token's own price on Solana is the reference. Search by name, ticker or issuer; filter by stocks or ETFs, by issuer, or to the markets with an OPmode pool; and sort by the day's move or by the gap. Beside the list are the five tokens that moved most in a day and the five furthest from their share.

A stock's page

What each part shows

1 · Bar
Markets and Pools, a search for any stock (/ or Ctrl K from any page), a link to the launchpad, and your wallet. Signed in, your address opens your wallet page; a dot on it means launch rewards are waiting.
2 · Price and gap
The token's name with a market picker, its issuer and the share it tracks, the price per share and the 24-hour move. Beside them, how far the token sits above or below its share, drawn from 2% under to 2% over, where the token's on-chain price is the reference; otherwise the share price it is priced from.
3 · Session
The US market session of the underlying share and when it changes next, from Pyth's calendar. A missing or stale calendar is labelled.
4 · Chart
Recorded reference prices over one hour, four hours or a day, with the share's price as a dashed line and the US sessions drawn underneath. Move across it to read a price and time. It labels any earlier venue history.
5 · About
How many shares one token stands for (the multiplier, which dividends and splits change), where the price comes from, and the token itself: issuer, token program, decimals, the mint check, the mint address and its extensions. Stocks that launch tokens can be paired with also list those launches.
6 · Ticket
Buy or Sell, the amount under You pay (a buy pays in USDC or SOL), and what you get, estimated, with the price per share against the share. Then the two routes, the one that takes the order marked. The first quote of a wallet asks for the product disclosures, the country of residence and the issuer acknowledgement, once. A Quick review shows estimated output, minimum received and the price per share; a buy of a share both issuers list also prices the other issuer's token. An OPmode review shows the exact output, its distance from the reference, the fee and an expiry of up to 10 seconds. The seconds left sit on the sign button.

Pools and the wallet

Pools
What the pools hold and what providers keep, your positions with Deposit and Withdraw on each, and every pool with its value, holdings, mix and your share. A pool's own page shows its value and mix, your position, a ticket to deposit both assets at the pool's mix or withdraw a share of your position, how the pool earns, and your deposits and withdrawals there. Pools, inventory and skew.
Wallet
One page for the terminal and the launchpad: the wallet's total, launch rewards to claim, every holding (stock tokens, launch tokens, pool shares, SOL and USDC) with its value, and activity from both products. See Wallet.
