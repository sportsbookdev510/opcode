Understand the price

Sessions and market hours

A stock token can trade beyond the underlying share's regular hours. opcode labels verified sessions and requires a fresh Pyth price in an open session for an OPmode quote.

The sessions

Each stock's page shows the session of the underlying market under the price, with a countdown to the next change. All times are New York time and follow daylight saving there.

SessionTypical hours, Monday to FridayWhat it means for you
US regular session09:30 – 16:00OPmode requires a Pyth source at most five seconds old, with confidence at most 25 bps of price.
US extended hours04:00 – 09:30 and 16:00 – 20:00A verified open session and a Pyth source at most five seconds old are required; confidence is capped at 50 bps.
US overnight session20:00 – 04:00OPmode needs explicit overnight coverage and a fresh matching Pyth source; confidence is capped at 50 bps.
US market closedWeekends and exchange holidaysNo new OPmode quotes. Quick remains dependent on its available routes.

OPmode requires an open session confirmed by both the calendar and the Pyth report. Its configured session term is zero, but confidence contributes to the spread, so a night-time quote need not match one at noon. Quick depends on the liquidity its route finds. The terminal shows the quote before you commit.

Holidays and early closes

The hours above are the usual ones. Exchanges close for holidays and shut early on a few days each year, such as the day after Thanksgiving. opcode does not work from a fixed clock. It takes each market's published schedule, holidays and early closes included, refreshes it daily, and labels the day when it is unusual: Holiday, Early close today, Weekend.

If the calendar cannot be trusted, there is no reference price

A schedule is fresh for two days. Public market data can use a delayed schedule for up to a week, but OPmode requires it to remain fresh. After a week the terminal shows Calendar unavailable. Missing or stale verified coverage stops new OPmode quotes until a fresh schedule arrives.

Planning around it

  • If you want the underlying share trading while you trade the token, the regular session is the time. Each stock's page shows the session and its next change.
  • An OPmode quote fixes its amounts for up to 10 seconds. Its expiry is also capped by the next verified session change.
  • Around earnings, most of the news arrives outside the regular session. That is exactly when the reference is least certain and the token can drift furthest from the share.
Go deeperWhy the hour matters to a pool

When you buy from an OPmode pool, it is left holding less stock and more USDC, and it carries that position until other trades bring it back. The pool is not hedged elsewhere, so a move in the share while New York is shut lands on the pool's liquidity providers. The engine can add a session premium for that risk; the ten live pools set it to zero. The litepaper and Chapter 4 of the whitepaper set out how the risk is measured.
