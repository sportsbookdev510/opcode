The assets

Markets

109 markets on Solana, each a stock token against USDC, and Robinhood's stock tokens on Robinhood Chain against USDG. The token's address is its identity. Check it, not the ticker, whenever you move these tokens outside opcode.

Tickers are not unique on Solana

Anyone can create a token called NVDAx. The addresses below come from the issuer's own registry and are the only ones opcode trades. What you are buying explains how each is re-checked on chain.

Robinhood Chain

Every token Robinhood lists as active in its own token list is a market, with its contract address under About on its page. They trade against USDG. Where a share is also listed on Solana, Markets shows it in the same row as the xStocks and Backpack tokens.

Routes

Listed xStocks and Backpack Securities tokens use Quick when a route is available. OPmode, a firm quote from opcode's own pool, has ten original xStocks markets: NVDAx, SPYx, QQQx, GLDx, TSLAx, AAPLx, MSFTx, METAx, COINx and CRCLx. These OPmode markets are currently paused. Further xStocks or Backpack pools require explicit setup, funding and verified reference support. Pools shows which pools are configured and what they currently allow. Placing a swap has their order limits, and How a quote is built their pricing.

Robinhood tokens swap through KyberSwap on Robinhood Chain, signed by an Ethereum wallet. Their price is the share's price from Robinhood, and the ticket's estimate is the token's own price on Robinhood Chain.

A market is priced only while its reference passes every check, so at any moment some markets can show no price. Reference prices explains when that happens.

How markets are chosen

A market is listed when the token exists in the issuer's registry, its on-chain configuration matches what opcode supports, and a schedule for its underlying exchange can be verified. While its mint fails a check, or its last verified schedule is more than a week old, the market shows the reason and no price.
