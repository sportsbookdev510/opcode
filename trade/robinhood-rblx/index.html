Disclosures

  1. Third-party tokensOpcode lists xStocks and Backpack Securities tokens. These are separate issuers and separate tokens, even when they reference the same stock. Ownership rights, redemption, dividends and transfer restrictions depend on the issuer's terms. Check the issuer and token address before trading.
  2. Trading involves riskPrices and liquidity can change, and you can lose funds. In Quick, review expected output, minimum received, slippage and fees. In OPmode, review exact input, output, fee and expiry when a quote is available. Liquidity providers hold pool shares and receive the pool's current asset composition when withdrawing, with possible gains or losses and no fixed yield.
  3. Your signature authorizes real fundsTrading uses real wallet funds and requires your transaction signature. Network fees may apply even if a transaction fails. Signing in proves wallet ownership; it does not authorize a swap.
  4. Dollar pricing assumptionStock-referenced OPmode prices treat 1 USDC as $1. This is a pricing assumption, not a guarantee of USDC's market value. New quotes may pause if USDC moves outside the supported parity range.
  5. Check the outcome before retryingA delayed response can leave a submitted transaction pending. Check the saved order's status before creating a replacement.
