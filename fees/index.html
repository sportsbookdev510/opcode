Fees

Review the fee token, quoted amounts and any network costs before signing. Quick carries no opcode platform fee; OPmode charges 5 bps of the input.

1 / A /

Quick fees

opcode's platform fee on Quick is 0%. The trading fee shown is the route's own fee from Jupiter; it varies by route and may include costs recovered for sponsored transactions. Review the complete quote rather than assuming a fixed rate.

Compare expected output and minimum received after the quoted trading costs. Network fees and account rent may also apply.

One basis point is 0.01%. Review the fee amount and token shown with your order.

1 / B /

OPmode fees

OPmode shows the exact trading fee in your input token. That fee is included in the input amount, not added on top.

The fee is 5 bps (0.05%) of the input on all ten OPmode markets: NVDAx, SPYx, QQQx, GLDx, TSLAx, AAPLx, MSFTx, METAx, COINx and CRCLx. 80% of it stays in the pool for liquidity providers and 20% goes to the protocol, whose fee revenue funds $OP buybacks. Review the exact input, output, fee and expiry on each quote.

The fee rounds up to the next whole token atom. An order whose remaining input or output is zero is rejected before settlement.

1 / C /

Price and slippage

Quick prices can move between quote and execution. Slippage tolerance, 0.50% on Quick, sets the acceptable change in output; it is not an extra fee. Minimum received is shown separately from expected output.

OPmode quotes an exact output with an expiry of up to 10 seconds. Its spread is reflected in that output, separately from the stated input fee. A quote can be rejected if it expires or market conditions no longer permit settlement. See how pricing works.

1 / D /

Network costs

A transaction may require SOL for network fees and new token-account rent. Some quotes sponsor certain costs; review the payment details before signing. A failed on-chain transaction may still incur network fees.

Wallet sign-in only signs a message and has no network fee. Signing in does not authorize a trade or spend your funds.

Trading uses real wallet funds and requires your transaction signature. Token availability is subject to issuer restrictions.
